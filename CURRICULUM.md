# Curriculum Specification

## 1. Curriculum philosophy

The learner should not follow a beginner-style chapter sequence.

The curriculum should be organized into parallel tracks:

1. Advanced Vocabulary
2. Advanced Grammar
3. Reading
4. Kanji
5. Medical Vocabulary
6. Clinical Communication
7. Clinical Cases

Each day can mix multiple tracks.

---

# 2. Advanced Japanese track

## A. Vocabulary

Target categories:

- abstract nouns,
- formal written vocabulary,
- academic language,
- news language,
- workplace language,
- connective expressions,
- stance expressions,
- evaluation terms,
- cause/effect vocabulary,
- social topics,
- science and health vocabulary.

Recommended target:
- N2 core vocabulary first,
- then high-frequency N1 vocabulary,
- then domain-specific medical Japanese.

---

## B. Grammar

Organize by function rather than only JLPT level.

### Contrast and concession
- ものの
- ながら
- にもかかわらず
- とはいえ
- とはいうものの

### Limitation
- にすぎない
- しかない
- にほかならない

### Evaluation and certainty
- に違いない
- はずだ
- ものだ
- わけだ

### Negative nuance
- わけではない
- とは限らない
- どころではない

### Cause and basis
- ことから
- ため
- ゆえに
- によって

### Change and correlation
- につれて
- に伴って
- に応じて
- とともに

### Formal written patterns
- において
- に関して
- に対して
- をめぐって
- を通じて
- に基づいて

# 3. Reading curriculum

## Stage 1: Structure recognition

Train:
- topic sentence,
- paragraph role,
- connectors,
- reference words,
- contrast.

## Stage 2: Paraphrase recognition

Train:
- synonymous reformulation,
- abstract restatement,
- negative paraphrase,
- cause/effect equivalence.

## Stage 3: Inference

Train:
- implied opinion,
- writer attitude,
- unstated conclusion,
- contextual meaning.

## Stage 4: Speed

Train:
- timed passage reading,
- selective rereading,
- question-first strategy as optional mode,
- keyword location.

## Stage 5: Long-form reading

Train:
- multi-paragraph essays,
- workplace texts,
- scientific explanations,
- health information,
- opinion pieces.

---

# 4. Reading difficulty design

Each passage should have metadata:

- level,
- characters,
- estimated reading time,
- vocabulary density,
- grammar density,
- inference difficulty,
- topic familiarity.

Suggested difficulty score:
1 to 10.
